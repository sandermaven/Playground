Who Is Dave?

Born in the frozen wilderness of Canada, David Duparc emerged into this world holding an invisible hockey stick in one hand and pointing at the sky with the other. Despite having the rhythmic coordination of a newborn moose on ice, Dave discovered early on that dancing isn't about skill — it's about commitment. Today, he is widely regarded as the most enthusiastically terrible disco dancer the world has ever witnessed. And he wouldn't have it any other way.

"Signature" Moves

(Technically these are all just the same move with different names)

  • 🏒
    The Hockey Shuffle
    It's just skating footwork. On a dance floor. Without skates. People have called ambulances thinking he was having a medical episode.
  • ⚡
    The Confident Flail
    Arms moving in ways that defy both rhythm AND physics. But he maintains eye contact the whole time. Terrifying. Inspiring.
  • 🔥
    The Leiden Lunge
    A move he invented at Headlights. It's just a regular lunge, but he does it while pointing at the DJ with both fingers. Every. Single. Time.
  • 🌊
    The Canadian Apology
    When Dave accidentally bumps into someone while dancing (which is constantly), he does a little bow. This has been incorporated into the routine.
  • 🚀
    The Vertical Enthusiasm
    Dave jumps. That's it. That's the move. But he jumps with such CONVICTION that people assume it must be intentional.
  • 💎
    The Duparc Freeze
    When the music stops, Dave freezes in whatever position he's in. Once he was stuck pointing at the ceiling for 47 seconds. Legendary.

The Legend Begins

1976

Born in the Great White North

David Duparc enters the world in Canada. The nurses note he was born with an unusual amount of confidence and a complete lack of rhythm. A prophetic combination.

1982

Discovers Ice Hockey

Young Dave straps on his first pair of skates. Finally, a sport where moving awkwardly at high speeds is ENCOURAGED. He thrives.

1994

First Dance Attempt

At a school dance, Dave tries to apply hockey strategies to the dance floor. "If I just skate around everyone really fast, they'll think I'm good." They did not.

Early 2000s

The Great Migration

Dave moves from Canada to the Netherlands. The Dutch, famously direct, immediately tell him his dancing is terrible. Dave interprets this as a challenge.

The Leiden Era

Headlights Student Decor

Dave discovers Headlights, the legendary student association in Leiden. Here, among fellow enthusiasts, he learns the sacred truth: it's not about dancing WELL, it's about dancing WITH CONVICTION.

Present Day

The Attitude Era

At nearly 50, Dave has achieved something few dancers ever do: complete immunity to embarrassment. His moves haven't improved. His attitude is immaculate. The dance floor is his kingdom.

Dave's Disco Philosophy

They told me I couldn't dance. They were right. But they never said I had to stop.

— David Duparc, Leiden, after being asked to leave a dance circle (2019)

🏒

Rule #1

Dance like you're on ice. This works for Dave because he literally thinks he's always on ice.

✨

Rule #2

If you can't be good, be memorable. Anyone can dance well. It takes a special person to dance badly with style.

🇨🇦

Rule #3

Always apologize. It's the Canadian way. "Sorry" is just part of the choreography.

💡

Rule #4

Headlights taught me: the dance floor doesn't judge. And if it does, you can't hear it over the music anyway.
