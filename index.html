Meet Bassie

Accredited Practitioner 🏒 Dutch Men's Hockey Team Manager

Bassie brings a unique blend of athletic discipline and healing intuition. As an accredited bodywork practitioner and manager of the Netherlands men's national hockey team, she understands the body like few others — from elite sport performance to deep somatic healing.

"Whether on the hockey pitch or in the treatment room, I believe in the power of presence, trust, and the body's extraordinary ability to heal."

With over twelve years of experience in tantric bodywork and somatic healing, combined with her role supporting the Dutch hockey squad through FIH Pro League and World Cup campaigns, Bassie brings a grounded, professional approach to every session. Trained in Amsterdam and Bali in tantric massage, trauma-informed touch, and breathwork, she combines ancient wisdom with athletic insight.

Her practice in Oud-Zuid is a warm, private sanctuary designed for your complete comfort and confidentiality. Whether you are exploring this work for the first time or deepening an existing practice, she meets you exactly where you are — with warmth, respect, and zero judgement.
