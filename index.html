The Legend Begins

1954

A Star Is Born (Literally Glowing)

Born during a thunderstorm directly above a discotheque. The doctor noted he came out doing jazz hands.

1962

First Dance Battle Victory

At age 8, Dave defeated a 35-year-old dance instructor in a neighborhood competition. Prize: a pair of gold-plated platform shoes.

1971

The Afro Arrives

Dave's iconic afro reaches peak magnificence. Scientists later classify it as its own gravitational field.

1977

Saturday Night Fever? No. Saturday Night DAVE.

Dave walks into Studio 54 and doesn't leave for 72 hours straight. Sets the all-time dance floor endurance record.

1982

The Disco Preservation Movement

When the world tries to cancel disco, Dave launches the "Funk Never Dies" campaign. He is arrested twice for illegal grooviness.

1995

Dave Goes Global

World tour across 47 countries. Credited with causing a nationwide dance revolution in at least 12 of them.

2024

Still Groovin'

At 70 years young, Dave shows no signs of slowing. His latest move, "The Time Warp Hustle," has gone viral with 200M views.
